# Nine-tier research progression

The shared engineering license cap is **Tech IX**. Campaign and PvP use exactly
the same catalog and vessel-fitting definitions, but separate authorization:
campaign technology is purchased and completed; skirmish and PvP cap the same
technology entries without awarding any campaign unlocks.

## Hull and specialization access

The previous hull-size reorganization is unchanged: the eleven **raw hull
families** unlock by Tech III (interceptors/corvettes at 0, escorts at I,
cruisers at II, carriers and dreadnoughts at III). Specialist strike/torpedo
variants begin at IV, command/EW at V, and logistics/repair at VI.
Tech VII-IX improve the weapons, machinery, defenses, supports, crew/habitat
modules, equipment and fittings available to any eligible hull. Higher ranks
do not conjure free modules or larger hulls.

## Campaign pacing across 500 missions

| I | 0 | 25,000 |
| II | 0 | 75,000 |
| III | 0 | 250,000 |
| IV | 0 | 900,000 |
| V | 0 | 3,500,000 |
| VI | 0 | 15,000,000 |
| VII | 250 | 28,000,000 |
| VIII | 350 | 45,000,000 |
| IX | 425 | 70,000,000 |

The first six licenses retain their previously established funding and
mission-work requirements. Milestones apply **only** to VII-IX. Existing
laboratory acceleration remains optional; higher-level module projects still
require their own funded research, a legal prerequisite chain and the necessary
successful missions. Tech IX becomes available early enough to be used during
the last 75 campaign missions, rather than after the campaign has ended.

## Real technology

The 374 physical component families retain all 4 exclusive specialization
branches across all 11 factions. The catalog now offers **9** stages per
branch: 374 x 4 x 9 = **13,464** research entries. The legacy first six
stages retain their source data. Stages VII-IX are created from the same
component's existing V/VI engineering modifiers with explicit predecessor
IDs, proportional cost growth and bounded physical tradeoffs. This is not a
separate tech tree or a generic percentage buff.

All module save/loads, campaign funded discovery, skirmish challenge codecs,
shipyard editing, and multiplayer tier authorization now accept stages I-IX.

## Verification

The continuous functional release gates on catalog, mission-economy, campaign
technology, shipyard lineage and skirmish technology tests in addition to
combat, multiplayer, and install/runtime tests. Hardware and performance are
not certified by a passing simulator test.
