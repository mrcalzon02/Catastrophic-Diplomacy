# Chapter openings, front briefings and sector-capital aftermaths
**Recording draft v1 · all ten chapters and all fifty fronts · each front spans ten missions**

These are skippable narrative interstitials, not routine voiced combat communications. A **chapter opening** plays before the first mission of its sector; a **front briefing** plays before that front's first mission; a **front resolution** plays only after that front's tenth mission is won. Chapter conclusions play after sector capitals (50, 100, ... 500). Do not play all three transitions in immediate succession if doing so slows the menus: offer a single 'story recap' control instead. The first ten missions and final seven have separate expanded recording scripts; avoid replaying duplicated introductory lines when a detailed scene exists.
