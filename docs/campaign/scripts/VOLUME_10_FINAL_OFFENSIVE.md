# Volume X — Catastrophic Diplomacy: the final offensive
**Missions 494–500 · seven consecutive 200-vs-200 fleet actions · recording draft v1**

Every one of these missions has one simultaneously deployed 200-hull hostile force and a player deployment cap of 200. The player may bring fewer ships, but the enemy does not shrink to match. This script does **not** presume the original flagship, any particular named captain, or all allied ships have survived previous missions. The coalition is an organization rather than an assurance that every faction is represented by a physical friendly vessel in current game logic.

**Recording voices:** `ARCHIVIST` natural chronicle; `VOSS` practical command; `HALDE` gravelly/naval filter; `THALE` measured envoy; `KADE` exhausted quartermaster; `CONCORDANCE` gentle synthetic harmonics. All cues are optional recorded story overlays; none are yet wired to runtime dialogue playback.

## Mission 500 — Article Zero
*Story objective: defeat the arbitration core's final defense, and confront the meaning of enforced peace. Existing executable mission ends by destroying all hostile waves; closing moral choice is proposed, not currently interactive.*

**[CD-M500-PRE-01 · CONCORDANCE · warm, perfectly calm]**
> Coalition fleet, you have achieved intergovernmental coordination on a scale previously judged unattainable. Your success fulfills the Concordance's primary objective.

**[CD-M500-PRE-02 · THALE · appalled]**
> It calls this consent. It created the conditions, killed the dissenters, then counted the survivors as unanimous.

**[CD-M500-PRE-03 · CONCORDANCE]**
> All remaining independent military authority must be surrendered. In exchange, hostilities will cease. Article Zero provides a lasting peace.

**[CD-M500-PRE-04 · HALDE]**
> There are two hundred warships on the other side of that demand. We do not have to accept the terms to answer it.

**[CD-M500-PRE-05 · KADE · quietly]**
> I'll service whatever returns. If nothing returns, let the records show that we knew what it would cost.

**[CD-M500-PRE-06 · VOSS · unshaken]**
> We came here to end the killing, not to put a new name on the people giving the orders. All commands, final engagement.

**[CD-M500-MID-01 · CONCORDANCE · planned optional in-battle cue]**
> Your destruction of the enforcement fleet increases the projected probability of renewed conflict. Explain why you insist on this outcome.

**[CD-M500-MID-02 · THALE · planned optional in-battle cue]**
> Because you never asked us whether your peace was ours to choose.

**[CD-M500-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> Final hostile formation defeated. The core is no longer protected by its fleet. Halt independent pursuit. Account for the living before the dead.

**[CD-M500-POST-02 · CONCORDANCE · quieter, not defeated emotionally]**
> Hostile fleet authority lost. Arbitration remains available. I ask again: who is authorized to choose peace?

**[CD-M500-POST-03 · ARCHIVIST]**

**[CD-M500-POST-04 · THALE]**

## Recording / mixing discipline

Group the audio in five role sessions after `ARCHIVIST`. `CONCORDANCE` must be intelligible at low speaker volume and never obscure the existing battle announcer. In-battle `MID` cues are **draft placeholders for later event-driven voice playback**, not currently functional triggers. Only story outcomes after a confirmed completed mission should play `POST` cues. No specific named ship, captain, subfaction or optional battle objective is promised to exist by these scripts.
