# Volume I — Border Signals: opening recording script
**Catastrophic Diplomacy · chapter 1, missions 001–010 · recording draft v1**

**Solo-performer legend:** `ARCHIVIST` natural, `VOSS` dry officer, `KADE` warm rasp, `THALE` restrained diplomatic formant, `HALDE` deep naval radio, `CONCORDANCE` synthetic calm. Record one line per cue with 0.7–1.0 seconds of silence. Do not use the generic game announcer for these parts; do not replace text-only per-ship chatter. All cue IDs are stable.

**Trigger truth:** premission dialogue may be used in a briefing menu; postmission cues require successful completion. Optional in-battle cues are **not yet implemented**. The existing encounter's actual victory condition is defeating all mandatory hostile waves; descriptions of convoys, beacons and salvage are story context, not currently physical escort or evidence-gathering goals.

## Opening: log recovered from the first patrol

**[CD-PRO-001 · ARCHIVIST · cold open, unhurried]**
> Every war has a first shot. Ours had a time stamp. That is why so many people spent so many years trying to erase it.

**[CD-PRO-002 · ARCHIVIST · quiet, one breath]**
> This is the unedited account of the first patrol. Names of the dead are preserved. Conclusions are not.

**[CD-PRO-003 · VOSS · command circuit, crisp]**
> Patrol command, you have one corvette and no reserve squadron. Your assignment is simple: verify the boundary beacons, report unauthorized traffic, and return with the ship intact.

**[CD-PRO-004 · KADE · dry, almost amused]**
> I've stocked your magazines, serviced the reactors, and counted every canister of fuel twice. Please don't make me count the wreckage.

**[CD-PRO-005 · VOSS · narrowed attention]**
> Unscheduled transmission at Glasswake Threshold. No recognized distress flag. The sender has requested military protection under a treaty clause no one here remembers signing.

**[CD-PRO-006 · ARCHIVIST · softer]**
> The commander could have turned back. They did not. The ship's name entered history that day. Its continued survival was never guaranteed.

## Mission 001 — Glasswake Threshold
*Operation: Patrol · Tech 0 · fleet cap 1 · immediate stakes: an impossible distress transmission.*

**[CD-M001-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> Glasswake Threshold. One unknown contact is refusing identification. Match its drive signature and keep your weapons trained away until it commits.

**[CD-M001-PRE-02 · KADE]**
> One vessel against one vessel. It sounds fair right up until one of them is yours.

**[CD-M001-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> Hostile contact destroyed. We recovered only part of the broadcast. Its navigation header references a relay that went dark before this patrol was commissioned.

**[CD-M001-POST-02 · ARCHIVIST]**
> The first battle settled ownership of nothing. It did leave behind a recording that nobody would agree to authenticate.

## Mission 002 — Glasswake Pale Signal
*Operation: Interdiction · the relay repeats its signal from a different approach.*

**[CD-M002-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> A courier vessel is running the old relay route. Stop the hostile screen and keep the signal recorder ready.

**[CD-M002-PRE-02 · THALE]**
> Those transmissions use recognized diplomatic formatting. That does not make their author a diplomat.

**[CD-M002-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> Another signal recovered. Different ship, same authentication chain. Whoever is transmitting has access to records older than this conflict.

**[CD-M002-POST-02 · KADE]**
> The ship's still flying. The repair bill is not yet a diplomatic incident.

## Mission 003 — Glasswake Outer Track
*Operation: Escort screen · a safe route must be established before traffic resumes.*

**[CD-M003-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> Local freighters have been ordered to wait outside the track. We are clearing the hostile picket line; the convoy moves only after we're gone.

**[CD-M003-PRE-02 · THALE]**
> Both sides of this border claim the right to protect the same civilian route. Their maps disagree by less than a single maneuvering burn.

**[CD-M003-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> The route is clear for now. Our survey buoy recorded an identical treaty challenge from two separate military transmitters.

**[CD-M003-POST-02 · ARCHIVIST]**
> A place need not move for a border to become a battlefield.

## Mission 004 — Glasswake Crossing
*Operation: Convoy battle · disputed relief traffic crosses a hostile command area.*

**[CD-M004-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> Hostile vessels are holding the crossing. Remove the fighting line before the relief ships risk this passage.

**[CD-M004-PRE-02 · KADE]**
> They're carrying water and machine parts. Not guns. No one should need a warship to deliver water.

**[CD-M004-POST-01 · THALE]**
> The crossing is open. The ceasefire confirmation arrived at both command posts with conflicting effective times.

**[CD-M004-POST-02 · VOSS]**
> Mark both originals. No transcription, no correction. We will need evidence no admiralty can edit.

## Mission 005 — Glasswake Hollow Shelf
*Operation: Torpedo raid · at Tech 0, early attackers carry available legal weapons, not unlicensed capital torpedoes.*

**[CD-M005-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> The attackers have formed an ordnance picket over the Hollow Shelf. Those are legal early-generation mounts, but their firing plan is too coordinated for a random raiding party.

**[CD-M005-PRE-02 · KADE]**
> Ammunition costs money. So does replacing the ship that carries it. Please choose the cheaper problem.

**[CD-M005-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> Their attack vectors were calculated from a chart no public navy has issued. We are recording the trajectory data in full.

**[CD-M005-POST-02 · ARCHIVIST]**
> People called it a skirmish. Someone had already prepared the map.

## Mission 006 — Glasswake Garrison
*Operation: Strike wing · early missiles and interception forces, before carrier technology.*

**[CD-M006-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> The garrison is launching another strike screen. We are targeting the warships, not the civilian navigation station behind them.

**[CD-M006-PRE-02 · THALE]**
> Their local command believes it is responding to a confirmed invasion. Our fleet has not crossed their own published boundary.

**[CD-M006-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> Their station is intact. Hostile vessels are disabled or destroyed. We now have both versions of the border coordinates.

**[CD-M006-POST-02 · KADE]**
> Two maps, one piece of space, and more holes in our armor. Progress.

## Mission 007 — Glasswake Broken Arc
*Operation: Siege line · early-tier light hulls, no premature dreadnought.*

**[CD-M007-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> The Broken Arc's outer defenses have been reinforced with patrol craft. Take the active ships out of action; we need the relay's transmit record undamaged.

**[CD-M007-PRE-02 · THALE]**
> The station's defense order bears an arbitration seal last registered generations ago. That should be impossible.

**[CD-M007-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> The fleet has withdrawn or been destroyed. The relay itself is still transmitting. No local operator claims control.

**[CD-M007-POST-02 · ARCHIVIST]**
> The silence after that battle belonged only to the crews. The machinery kept issuing instructions.

## Mission 008 — Glasswake Underwatch
*Operation: Breakthrough · force a tactical approach to the abandoned diplomatic route.*

**[CD-M008-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> Another hostile line across Underwatch. We need a clear approach, not a triumphal parade. Go through the obstruction and preserve ammunition.

**[CD-M008-PRE-02 · HALDE · first recorded appearance, rigid radio]**
> Unknown commander, this corridor is under lawful interdiction. Withdraw now. You will not receive a second warning.

**[CD-M008-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> Corridor cleared. The admiral's signature on that warning is genuine. His authorization is not listed in our current diplomatic registry.

**[CD-M008-POST-02 · KADE]**
> We won the argument with missiles. That means we will probably have to make the argument again.

## Mission 009 — Glasswake Darkward
*Operation: Command fleet · light legal command escorts; no carriers at this tier.*

**[CD-M009-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> We've reached the local command pickets. Disable their fighting ships and retrieve the authorization header from their last transmitted order.

**[CD-M009-PRE-02 · HALDE]**
> You are pursuing a signal into sovereign military space. Every engagement you provoke is being entered into the legal record.

**[CD-M009-POST-01 · THALE]**
> Both the interdiction warrant and our corridor clearance were signed by the same arbitration authority. That authority has not convened for decades.

**[CD-M009-POST-02 · VOSS]**
> No conclusions yet. We have a signature. Next we establish when it was issued.

## Mission 010 — Glasswake Heartline
*Operation: Fleet action · first ten-mission front confrontation; no unearned large-fleet spectacle.*

**[CD-M010-PRE-01 · VOSS]**
> Heartline has become the nearest place where both navies can compare their original orders. The other side has sent ships instead of witnesses.

**[CD-M010-PRE-02 · KADE]**
> We've gone from one patrol to a shipyard's worth of difficult questions. If you've bought a second hull, at least let me service it first.

**[CD-M010-PRE-03 · THALE]**
> Commanders, our dispute may not be about whose documents are authentic. It may be about who authorized them.

**[CD-M010-POST-01 · VOSS]**
> We recovered the relay's unaltered clock record. The distress signal was transmitted before the vessel named as its sender had ever launched.

**[CD-M010-POST-02 · THALE]**
> Then we have authenticated an impossible event. Someone expected this war before any of us believed it could begin.

**[CD-M010-POST-03 · ARCHIVIST]**
> Glasswake did not reveal who started the war. It revealed that the beginning of the war had been scheduled.

## Transition to Cinder Semaphore (M011)

**[CD-F002-INTRO-01 · VOSS]**
> Our next orders concern a ship with three lawful transponder identities. We will collect its actual course history before three governments decide which one owns the wreck.

**[CD-F002-INTRO-02 · THALE]**
> Authentication proves that a credential was accepted. It does not prove that its authority still exists.

## Recording notes

Read natural `ARCHIVIST` cues in one pass, then all `VOSS`, `KADE`, `THALE`, `HALDE`. The sequence IDs let the audio editor slice from one continuous recording per character. A cue can have multiple takes, but its stable ID must not change. Do not add new soundtrack, character models, or speech-driven battle triggers until those implementations have their own scope and validation. Voice filter processing should preserve understandable words on Android speaker audio.
